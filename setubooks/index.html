How it works

Two small pieces. Your data moves directly between your own systems.

  1. 1

    Install the ERPNext app

    It adds mapping screens (ledgers ↔ accounts, parties, items, GST tax templates, cost centres), a sync queue and a sync log with retry for failed entries.

  2. 2

    Run the small Windows bridge next to Tally

    Tally only listens on the PC where it runs, so a lightweight tray app on that machine talks to Tally locally and to your ERPNext site over HTTPS. There's nothing to expose to the internet.

  3. 3

    Vouchers and ledgers sync

    Sales/purchase invoices, payments and journals go to Tally as vouchers with the right GST ledgers. Ledgers and parties are kept in step on both sides.

  4. 4

    Reconcile before you file

    A period-wise report compares trial balance, GST and party balances in ERPNext vs Tally and lists exactly which vouchers differ.

FAQ

Is this a migration tool?

No. The first goal is co-existence: keep ERPNext for operations and Tally for books, in sync. A guided Tally → ERPNext migration may follow for those who want to move fully.

Which versions will be supported?

The plan is TallyPrime on Windows and current ERPNext versions (v15 and v16), both self-hosted and cloud-hosted sites. Tell us your versions in the form and we'll confirm before beta.

Why does it need a Windows app?

Tally's integration interface runs on the computer where Tally is installed, so a cloud server can't reach it directly. The small bridge app runs there, connects to Tally locally, and talks to ERPNext over HTTPS.

Where does my accounting data go?

Directly between your Tally PC and your ERPNext site. It is designed so your books are not stored on our servers. Only minimal licence and health information would be sent, and we will document this fully before launch.

Will my CA have to change anything?

No. Your CA keeps working in Tally as usual. Vouchers simply arrive already entered, with the GST ledgers you choose in the mapping.

What if an entry fails to sync?

It stays in the sync queue with a clear error (for example a missing ledger mapping). Fix the mapping and retry, so nothing is silently dropped.

When will it be available, and what does joining cost?

Joining is free. We're validating demand first: if enough businesses and CAs sign up, waitlist members get beta access first, with founding-member pricing.
