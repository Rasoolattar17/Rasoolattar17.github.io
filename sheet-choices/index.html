Edit choices once in a Sheet. Your form stays up to date.

Sheet Choices fills dropdown and multiple-choice options from a Google Sheet column and refreshes them on a schedule. If the sheet breaks, the add-on keeps the last good list and emails you instead of wiping your form blank.
