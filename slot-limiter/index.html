How it works

Three steps after launch. We ship when enough founding pre-orders come in (target ~2–4 weeks from that point).

  1. 1

    Install the add-on

    Open your Google Form and enable Slot Limiter from the add-ons menu (Google Workspace account required).

  2. 2

    Set limits per choice

    Pick the question and enter max seats for each option — or import limits from a sheet if you prefer.

  3. 3

    Share the form

    Full options disappear for new respondents; you get a restore button and a simple status view in the sidebar.

Transparency: Like every Google Forms limiter, two people submitting in the same instant might both pick the last seat before counts update. We minimize that, but we will not claim zero race conditions.
