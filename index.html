About Me

I'm Rasool Attar — a Senior Moodle Developer with 5 years of hands-on experience building enterprise learning platforms for organizations worldwide.

From custom plugins and branded themes to complex HRMS integrations and automated regression testing with LmsIQ, I deliver end-to-end Moodle solutions that scale. I specialize in turning complex LMS requirements into reliable, maintainable code.

LmsIQ — AI-ready LMS Regression Testing Platform

  • Moodle-aware actions — loginAs, openCourse, completeScorm
  • Semantic workflow recorder

  • GitHub Actions CI/CD integration
